# Support Handbook v3

**Date:** May 2, 2026  
**Author:** Sofia Bennett, VP of Customer Success

## Standard response targets

Enterprise customers have a standard support response target of **4 hours**.

Self-serve customers have a standard response target of **24 hours**.

## Refunds

Refunds above **$150** require manager approval.

Refunds below $150 may be approved by authorized support representatives.

## Production incidents

Urgent production incidents should be escalated immediately to the on-call engineering team.

This handbook was the active support policy before Support Handbook v4 was issued.
