# Q1 Marketing Plan

**Date:** January 20, 2026  
**Author:** Elena Petrova, VP of Marketing

## Budget

The Q1 marketing budget is **$35,000**.

## Planned allocation

- Paid search
- LinkedIn advertising
- Customer case studies
- Webinar production
- Marketing software

## Objectives

The primary objective is to generate qualified mid-market pipeline rather than maximize raw website traffic.

Target audiences include:

- Operations leaders
- Customer success leaders
- Revenue operations teams

## Channel strategy

LinkedIn advertising and paid search will provide initial demand generation. Customer case studies and webinars will provide proof and education.

Large-scale creator partnerships are not part of the Q1 plan.

Marketing will measure qualified pipeline, demo requests, and opportunity creation.
