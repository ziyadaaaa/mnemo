# Q3 Marketing Planning

**Date:** June 10, 2026  
**Author:** Elena Petrova, VP of Marketing

## Proposed Q3 budget

The proposed Q3 marketing budget is **$48,000**.

## Proposed allocation

| Category | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Paid social | $18,000 |
| Creator partnerships | $12,000 |
| Customer case studies | $7,000 |
| Webinars and events | $6,000 |
| Testing reserve | $5,000 |
| **Total** | **$48,000** |

## Rationale

The increase from the Q1 budget reflects lessons from Q1. Broad paid search produced lower-quality opportunities, while customer case studies produced a higher proportion of qualified leads.

The team recommends reducing broad paid search and increasing targeted activity.

The primary Q3 audience remains mid-market operations and customer success leaders.

Creator partnerships will be tested because established operators may provide credibility with the target audience.

The proposed budget requires executive approval.
