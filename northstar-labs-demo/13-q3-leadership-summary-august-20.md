# Q3 Leadership Summary

**Date:** August 20, 2026  
**Author:** Maya Chen, CEO

## Status

This document represents the **current authoritative executive direction as of August 20, 2026**.

It supersedes earlier strategic assumptions where they conflict with the priorities listed here.

## Current priorities

### 1. Enterprise retention

Northstar will prioritize reliability, support quality, and successful enterprise implementations.

### 2. Efficient growth

Marketing and Sales will focus on qualified mid-market and enterprise pipeline rather than maximizing top-of-funnel volume.

### 3. Product reliability and governance

Product and Engineering will prioritize workflow reliability, permissions, auditability, and security before expanding into additional product categories.

## Hiring

The company will maintain controlled hiring during Q3.

Engineering remains under a net-new hiring freeze.

Previously approved replacement positions continue.

## Marketing

The approved Q3 marketing budget is **$48,000**.

Paid social and creator partnerships receive priority.

Marketing should continue measuring qualified pipeline rather than relying primarily on impressions or broad traffic.

## Market expansion

Northstar will not pursue a consumer-market expansion during the remainder of 2026.

The company remains focused on business customers, particularly mid-market and enterprise organizations.
