# Company Overview and 2026 Operating Plan

**Date:** January 8, 2026  
**Author:** Maya Chen, CEO

## Executive overview

Northstar Labs entered 2026 with 74 employees and approximately $8.4M in annual recurring revenue. The company’s 2026 objective is to reach $12M ARR while improving gross retention and reducing the amount of manual work required to operate customer workflows.

## 2026 company priorities

1. Expand Northstar Flow’s workflow automation capabilities.
2. Improve enterprise customer retention.
3. Build a more efficient go-to-market organization.

The company expects most new revenue to come from mid-market customers rather than very small businesses.

## Product and engineering

Product and Engineering will focus on workflow reliability, integrations, permissions, and analytics. Reliability is considered particularly important for enterprise customers that depend on automated workflows for recurring operational processes.

## Marketing

Marketing will focus on demand generation, customer proof points, and educational content rather than broad brand advertising. The primary audiences are operations, customer success, and revenue operations leaders.

## Hiring and operations

The annual operating plan assumes controlled hiring. New positions require department-level justification and approval from the executive team.

The company intends to grow carefully while maintaining spending discipline.
