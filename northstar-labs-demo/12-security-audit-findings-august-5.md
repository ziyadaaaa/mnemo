# Security Audit Findings

**Date:** August 5, 2026  
**Author:** Marcus Reed, VP of Engineering

## Summary

The security audit identified several issues requiring additional validation before the Northstar Flow 2.4 release.

The findings did not indicate a confirmed customer data breach.

However, the audit identified permission edge cases that require additional testing.

## Engineering recommendation

Engineering recommended delaying final release approval until the security team completes regression testing across role and permission combinations.

The product team was asked not to treat September 5 as a guaranteed launch date.

The highest priority is validating that users cannot access workflow information outside their authorized permissions.

Additional regression testing is therefore required before final release approval.
