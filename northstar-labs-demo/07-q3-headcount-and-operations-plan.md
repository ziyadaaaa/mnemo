# Q3 Headcount and Operations Plan

**Date:** June 28, 2026  
**Author:** Daniel Ortiz, COO

## Headcount

Northstar enters Q3 with **82 employees**.

## Engineering

The company will temporarily freeze net-new engineering headcount during Q3 because Product and Engineering spending is already above the original annual plan.

The freeze does not apply to approved replacement hiring.

Two senior full-stack engineering positions have been approved as backfills for employees who left the company.

The backfill hiring target is **August 1, 2026**.

These positions are replacement roles rather than an expansion of engineering headcount.

## Other departments

Other departments may request new positions, but requests must be reviewed by Finance and approved by the executive team.

Customer Success may add one implementation specialist if enterprise customer volume exceeds the capacity assumptions in the operating plan.

Managers should continue to distinguish replacement hiring from net-new expansion when submitting requests.
