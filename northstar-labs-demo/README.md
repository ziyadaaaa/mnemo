# Northstar Labs Demo Dataset

Northstar Labs is a fictional synthetic company created exclusively for the public Mnemo demo.

The dataset contains historical and current company information across strategy, marketing, hiring, support, product, security, and customer research.

## Important temporal relationships

- The Q1 marketing budget was $35,000.
- The Q3 marketing budget was proposed at $48,000 and approved on June 14, 2026.
- The August 20 Q3 Leadership Summary confirms $48,000 as the current approved Q3 marketing budget.
- Engineering net-new hiring is frozen during Q3, while two previously approved senior full-stack replacement roles remain authorized.
- Support Handbook v4 supersedes Support Handbook v3. The enterprise response target changed from 4 hours to 2 hours.
- Northstar Flow 2.4 originally targeted September 5, 2026.
- The August 5 security audit required additional permission testing.
- The August 21 launch meeting revised the target to September 18, 2026.
- The August 20 Q3 Leadership Summary is the current authoritative executive strategy document.

All people, company information, customers, metrics, and events in this dataset are fictional and created for software demonstration purposes.
