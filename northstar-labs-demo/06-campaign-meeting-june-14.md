# Campaign Meeting Notes

**Date:** June 14, 2026  
**Author:** Elena Petrova, VP of Marketing

## Attendees

- Maya Chen, CEO
- Elena Petrova, VP of Marketing
- Daniel Ortiz, COO
- James Walker, VP of Sales
- Sofia Bennett, VP of Customer Success

## Decisions

The group reviewed the Q3 marketing proposal.

Maya Chen approved the proposed **$48,000 Q3 campaign budget**.

The team agreed that paid social and creator partnerships should receive priority.

Daniel Ortiz requested that the marketing team track qualified pipeline rather than impressions as the primary performance measure.

James Walker asked Marketing to coordinate campaign messaging with Sales so that the same customer problems are reflected in outbound campaigns.

Sofia Bennett offered two customer case studies for the Q3 campaign.

The group agreed that broad brand advertising should remain limited until the team has stronger evidence of its impact on qualified pipeline.
