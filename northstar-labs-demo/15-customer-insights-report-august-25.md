# Customer Insights Report

**Date:** August 25, 2026  
**Author:** Sofia Bennett, VP of Customer Success

## Summary

Customer interviews conducted during Q3 identified three recurring priorities.

First, enterprise customers want stronger administrative controls and visibility into workflow activity.

Second, customers want clearer explanations when automated workflows fail.

Third, larger customers want faster support responses and more structured implementation assistance.

## Product feedback

Several customers said that reliability and governance are more important than adding a large number of new integrations.

Customers responded positively to stronger auditability, permissions, and workflow diagnostics.

## Strategic implications

The feedback is consistent with Northstar’s current strategy of prioritizing enterprise retention, reliability, and governance.

The report recommends continuing to prioritize:

- Auditability
- Permissions
- Workflow diagnostics
- Enterprise support
- Structured implementation assistance

This report provides customer evidence supporting the executive direction documented in the August 20 Q3 Leadership Summary.
