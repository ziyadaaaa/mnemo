# Support Handbook v4

**Date:** July 10, 2026  
**Author:** Sofia Bennett, VP of Customer Success

## Status

**Support Handbook v4 supersedes Support Handbook v3.**

This is the current customer support policy as of July 10, 2026.

## Standard response targets

Enterprise customers now have a standard support response target of **2 hours**.

Self-serve customers retain a standard response target of **24 hours**.

## Refunds

Refunds above **$150** continue to require manager approval.

## Production incidents

The escalation process has been updated to require immediate notification of the Customer Success lead and the engineering on-call contact.

Enterprise customers experiencing a production-blocking issue should be escalated immediately.

The July 10 policy should be used when answering questions about the current support process.
