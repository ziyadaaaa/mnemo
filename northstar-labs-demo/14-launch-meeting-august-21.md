# Launch Meeting Notes

**Date:** August 21, 2026  
**Author:** Priya Raman, Chief Product Officer

## Northstar Flow 2.4 launch

The product leadership team reviewed the status of Northstar Flow 2.4.

The original target launch date was **September 5, 2026**.

That date is no longer achievable because additional security regression testing is required.

## Revised target

The revised target launch date is **September 18, 2026**.

The September 18 date is a target rather than a guarantee.

Security validation must be completed before release.

## Release scope

The release remains focused on:

- Workflow analytics
- Audit logs
- Permission controls
- Salesforce improvements
- Workflow failure diagnostics

The team explicitly rejected removing security testing requirements simply to preserve the original launch date.
