# 2026 Annual Product Strategy

**Date:** January 15, 2026  
**Author:** Priya Raman, Chief Product Officer

Northstar Flow’s 2026 product strategy is organized around four themes.

## 1. Reliability

Customers must be able to trust automated workflows to execute correctly and consistently.

## 2. Integrations

Northstar will expand the number and depth of integrations supported by the platform.

## 3. Governance

Enterprise customers need stronger permissions, auditability, and administrative controls.

## 4. Analytics

Customers should be able to understand how workflows are performing and where operational bottlenecks exist.

## Product sequencing

The first half of 2026 will prioritize platform reliability and integrations.

The second half will place greater emphasis on governance and analytics.

Northstar does not plan to pursue a broad expansion into consumer users in 2026. The product organization will remain focused on business customers.
