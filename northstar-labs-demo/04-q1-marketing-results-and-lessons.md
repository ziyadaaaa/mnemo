# Q1 Marketing Results and Lessons

**Date:** April 8, 2026  
**Author:** Elena Petrova, VP of Marketing

## Summary

Q1 marketing generated strong website traffic but produced fewer qualified opportunities than expected.

LinkedIn advertising generated awareness but had a relatively high cost per qualified opportunity.

Customer case studies generated fewer total visitors but a higher proportion of qualified leads.

## Lessons

The results suggest that customer-led content and targeted campaigns are more useful for qualified pipeline than broad awareness activity.

The team recommends shifting budget away from broad paid awareness toward customer-led content and targeted campaigns.

The report also recommends testing creator partnerships with operators who have established audiences among marketing and operations leaders.

The Q1 budget of $35,000 was considered sufficient for testing but insufficient for a larger multi-channel campaign.

These findings will inform the Q3 marketing plan.
